# The Kill Room

`original.webp` is the painting as supplied. `extend.py` trims its thin dark frame line, then widens it on both sides with more of its own plastic sheeting, ceiling, floor and front lip (image quilting from object-free parts of the painting), tones the new strips to the edges they continue and lets the far ends fall into shadow. The result is `stages/killroom.webp` (1375x572). The game draws it as one rigid piece at one game unit per pixel; fighters stand on row 480.
